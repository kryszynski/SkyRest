Inflight Rest Requirements (OM-A 7.2.1.2.(2)(e)):
  • Minimum consecutive rest: 90 minutes per crew member
  • For landing crew: Consecutive 3 hours for flight crew at controls during landing
  • Rest allocation: Optimized to ensure alertness at controls during landing
  • Wake-up calls: Set before rest period ends according to selected switchover time
  • Switchover time: Buffer period before rest end for wake-up and handover
  • Rest timing: Should be taken during cruise phase
  • PIC responsibility: Commander determines order and duration in consultation with crew
  • All time in rest facility counts as FDP

⚠ Commander's Discretion (OM-A 7.2.1.2.(2)(f))

Unforeseen circumstances starting at/after reporting:

  • Non-augmented crew: FDP may be extended by up to +2 hours
  • Augmented crew: FDP may be extended by up to +3 hours
  • On final sector: If unforeseen circumstances occur after takeoff, flight may continue to planned destination or alternate

Post-extension rest requirement: Minimum 12 hours rest must follow

Important: Commander must:

  • Consult all crew members on alertness levels before deciding
  • Contact LCC (Load Control Center)
  • Submit report on Journey Log describing circumstances
  • Consider WOCL encroachment, weather, complexity, crew condition, time since awake

⚠ This is a non-punitive process - safety takes precedence. If extension exceeds 1 hour, report must be sent to CAA within 28 days.
